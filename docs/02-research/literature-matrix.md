# Literature Matrix: Categorized Analysis of Research Foundations

This matrix presents a structured, verifiable analysis of the academic literature, preprints, and specifications underpinning the **Trust-Aware Service Selection for Autonomous AI Agents** research project.

To avoid misleading "apples-to-apples" comparisons across fundamentally different problem domains, every source is classified by its primary **Research Family**. Publication status is explicitly documented to maintain strict academic transparency.

# 30-Second Elevator Pitch

## The Script (Read out loud in 30 seconds)

> *"Autonomous AI agents are increasingly delegating tasks to external third-party services. But today's decentralized marketplaces are vulnerable: empirical studies show up to **90% of reviewers are coordinated Sybil bots**, and global star ratings don't tell you if an agent can handle your specific task.*
>
> *Our project introduces an **evidence-based trust and verification framework**. Instead of relying on easily gamed reputation, our system evaluates candidates using **verified, task-specific past performance**, validates outputs in isolated sandboxes, and anchors cryptographic audit logs.*
>
> *In short: we replace ungrounded reputation with verified empirical evidence so AI agents can delegate safely."*

---

## Key Numbers to Remember
- **90.6%**: Peak coordinated Sybil reviewers observed in real-world ERC-8004 registries.
- **3% to 15%**: Live, functional service endpoints in existing decentralized agent ecosystems.
- **30 Agents**: Our controlled testbed population (20 Reliable, 5 Unreliable, 5 Malicious).
- **3 Strategies**: Random vs. Reputation-Only vs. Evidence-Based.

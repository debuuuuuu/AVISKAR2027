## 2. Typography & Font Hierarchy (Readable at 2 Meters)

- **Main Title:** 72–80 pt, Bold Sans-Serif (Inter, Roboto, or Helvetica), High Contrast.
- **Subtitle:** 36–40 pt, Regular / Medium.
- **Section Headings:** 28–32 pt, Bold, with colored accent background bar.
- **Body Text:** 18–20 pt, 1.3 line spacing, High-readability Dark Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`) on White background.
- **Captions & Citations:** 14–16 pt, Muted Gray (`#555555`).

---

## 3. Color Palette & Visual Philosophy

- **Primary Brand Color (Trust / Security):** Deep Navy Blue (`#0F172A`)
- **Accent Color (Verification / Success):** Emerald Green (`#059669`)
- **Warning / Threat Accent (Sybil / Adversaries):** Crimson Red (`#DC2626`)
- **Background:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle off-white panel containers (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Design Philosophy:** **Information Density & Rigor > Fluff / Decoration**. The central architectural block diagram should be the visual centerpiece of the poster.
